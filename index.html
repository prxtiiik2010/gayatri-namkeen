Our Heritage

A Family Business Built on Trust

Decades of authentic craftsmanship & purity.

Founded in 1965, Gayatri Namkeen started as a humble family enterprise with a singular vision: to serve the most authentic, crispy, and flavorful namkeen. Over the years, we have preserved the original secret recipes passed down through generations.

Our dedication to quality means we only use 100% pure double-filtered oils and freshly stone-ground spices. From the classic Ratlami Sev to crunchy Gathiyas and savory Bhujias, our snacks carry the genuine taste of legacy in every single bite.
